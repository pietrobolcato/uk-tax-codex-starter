# Document collection checklist

Collect only items that existed in the relevant UK tax year. Prefer original PDFs plus machine-readable CSV/XLSX exports. Do not manually edit provider exports.

## Administration and residence

- Confirmation that a UTR exists and that the relevant return is active; do not store login credentials
- UK arrival/departure and midnight-presence calendar
- Tenancy, home-availability, immigration, or other residence evidence where relevant
- Prior-year residence history needed for the Statutory Residence Test or FIG eligibility
- Partner/child and UK-ties facts where relevant

## Employment and pensions

- P60 and any P45s
- Every payslip, including off-cycle bonus/equity payslips
- P11D, or confirmation that benefits were payrolled
- Employment contract/start or termination confirmation
- PAYE coding notices where relevant
- Workplace and personal-pension contribution statements

## Employee shares and overseas duties

- Grant agreements, vest schedules, vest confirmations, and payroll entries
- Sell-to-cover and sale confirmations, fees, and transaction exports
- Overseas-work diary with physical location, hours, purpose, travel status, employer, and evidence for each day
- Employer travel approvals, calendars, expenses, flights, and hotel records

## Banks and investments

- UK and foreign interest statements
- Brokerage annual/activity statements in PDF and CSV
- Complete trades, positions, dividends, withholding, fees, FX, and corporate actions
- Historic acquisition records for assets sold during the year
- Fund tax reports and excess reportable income information where relevant
- ISA statements sufficient to establish wrapper status and subscriptions

## Crypto and other sources

- Full exchange and wallet histories covering acquisitions, disposals, swaps, spending, rewards, and transfers
- Property, self-employment, freelance, royalty, trust, estate, bond, or private-company records
- Foreign tax paid or refunded
- Gift Aid, professional subscriptions, employment expenses, student loans, and Child Benefit facts
- Gifts or loans only where their nature or source-of-funds treatment needs documenting

## Final filing evidence

- Reviewed return and supplementary pages
- Tax calculation and scenario comparison
- Supporting computations and attachments
- Submission acknowledgement and final filed copy
